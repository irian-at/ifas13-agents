During the Fondspreise ledger discussion (2026-09-30) both option cards I raised were rejected
with "wants to clarify" — each time Mathias had a different shape in mind than any option
offered (uuids + a source table; one row per line with both version ids). What worked: a small
table of the alternatives with their costs, a concrete example (job/line/version letters with a
glossary), then a single open question.

**Why:** cards box his thinking into my buckets; he is still working out the shape and prefers
to state it himself. A rejected card costs a round trip and reads as pushing.

**How to apply:** in /discuss, present forks as prose or a compact table and ask one open
question; reserve cards for the rare fork where he has already said the options are exactly
these. Say back his design in a column table before questioning it. See also
[[feedback_glossary-for-symbols-and-refs]].
