When a discussion uses variable names (jobs O/Z/P, keys K, actions N/D, sources like `Z/5`) or
refers to numbered parts of a plan or review (B2, Chunk 3, §3.1), put a short glossary **upfront**
— before the argument — defining each symbol and reference as used in that message.

**Why:** Mathias reads these across sessions and days; a symbol introduced three messages earlier
or a "B2" from a review file is not in his head when he reads the next message. He asked for this
explicitly on 2026-09-25 during the preis_historie review discussion.

**How to apply:** a compact glossary block (table or bullets) at the top of the message, only the
symbols/references actually used in that message, one line each. Re-issue it in every message that
uses them, not just the first. Applies to chat replies and to plan/review files alike (see
[[plan-file-naming]] for where those live).
